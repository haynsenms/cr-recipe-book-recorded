# Beef Stew

## Ingredients

- Ox tail (~2 kg)
- Garlic (Half a head)
- Carrots (~5 medium)
- Potatoes (~3 medium)
- Tomato paste (2 Tbsp)
- Red wine (1 cup)
- Flour (1/2 cup)
- Beef stock
- Bay leaves (3-4)
- Thyme (1 tsp)
- Oregano (1/2 tsp)
- Salt
- Pepper
- Water

## Instructions 

- Salt and pepper the ox tail
- Bake ox tail on aluminum foil at 180'C for 40 minutes.
-   Fold the sides of the aluminum foil to contain the liquid fat
